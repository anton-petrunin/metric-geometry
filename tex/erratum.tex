\paragraph{page 54, line 8--11:} Change formulation of theorem to the following:

\paragraph{5.30 Theorem}{\textit Let $\spc{X}_n$ be a sequence of proper metric spaces with marked points $x_n\in \spc{X}_n$.
Assume that for any fixed $R>0$, the $R$-balls $\cBall(x_n,R)_{\spc{X}_n}$ form a uniformly totally bounded family.
Then a subsequence of $\spc{X}_n$ admits a Gromov--Hausdorff convergence such that the sequence of marked points $x_n\in\spc{X}_n$ converges.}
